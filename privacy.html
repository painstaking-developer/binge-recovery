Privacy Policy

Binge Recovery · Last updated October 2, 2026

Binge Recovery is a web player for recovery talks stored on Google Drive. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no Binge Recovery server, account system, database, analytics, or advertising.

Google sign-in and your Google data

To play a talk, you sign in with Google and grant read-only Drive access (drive.readonly). Google requires this because the talks are files on Google Drive, and downloading them through your own signed-in account is the only way Drive will serve them reliably. With that access, the app:

  • Downloads only the audio file of the talk you choose to play, straight from Google Drive to your browser.
  • Reads your Google account's email address once, so the next sign-in can pre-select your account instead of asking you to pick it again.

The app does not list, open, read, change, or delete any of your own Drive files.

What is stored, and where

  • The temporary Google access token (valid for about an hour) and your email address are kept only in your browser's local storage on your device.
  • Passwords you enter to unlock content are also saved only on your device.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. Your data is not shared, sold, or used for advertising.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Binge Recovery's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove Binge Recovery's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic request information (such as IP addresses) as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
